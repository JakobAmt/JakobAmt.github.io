Dumbpad Macropad

QMK Firmware in C Low Voltage Electronics Mechanical Assembly 3D Printing

A 3D-printed, hand-soldered macropad based on the open-source dumbpad v1.x dual-encoder design, built with a PCB and a Pro Micro (ATmega32u4) running QMK firmware.

The build combined mechanical assembly, embedded firmware development and basic electronics: soldering the switches, diode matrix and status LEDs (including resistors), wiring two rotary encoders, and programming a custom QMK keymap in C with help of QMK Developer tools.

Firmware

Custom QMK keymap and firmware logic:

  • Numpad layout on the key matrix
  • Encoder 1 mapped to system volume
  • Encoder 2 cycles an RGB indicator LED through on/off color combinations via GPIO

What I learned

  • 3D printing the case and enclosure
  • Hands-on soldering
  • Embedded C with QMK: the build system, GPIO control and encoder handling
